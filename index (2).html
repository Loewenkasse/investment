Guide for family

This page lists every investment the family holds. Each row shows what it is, where it is held and what it is worth today. Click a row to see details, notes, purchase history and past values.

Values are entered by hand unless noted otherwise. Open an investment, type the new value and save. The old value stays in its history.

Backups: press Export to download all data as a file. Keep copies in more than one place (cloud drive, USB stick, email to a relative). Import restores a file on any computer.

Sign-in details for banks, brokers and insurers should be kept in a password manager, never in this page. Use the Notes field to say where each account is and who to call.

Startup stakes are estimates and may not be sellable. Pension plans usually cannot be accessed before retirement age.
